# Numerology Methods

System: Pythagorean (primary) with Chaldean comparison. All arithmetic is
deterministic (`src/numerology`); letter-by-letter workings are exposed so
users can verify by hand (spec §13). No AI arithmetic.

## Core numbers

Life Path (three-group reduction, masters 11/22/33 preserved, karmic debts
13/14/16/19 flagged), Birthday, Expression, Soul Urge (vowels), Personality
(consonants), Maturity (LP + Expression).

## Extended

Karmic Lessons (missing 1–9 in name), Hidden Passion (most frequent value),
Balance (initials), Subconscious Self (9 − lesson count), Rational Thought
(first name + birth day), Cornerstone/Capstone/First Vowel, Chaldean
expression comparison, Essence cycles (letters active by value-duration),
Challenges (4), Pinnacles (4, first ends at 36 − LP), Period Cycles,
Personal Year/Month/Day.

## Y treatment

`auto` (vowel only when a word has no other vowel), or forced `vowel`/`consonant` —
configurable per calculation.

## Applied scoring

Name/brand/business scoring (favorable business numbers 1/3/5/8/22/33, karmic
debt penalty), name compatibility matrix, address/phone/plate vibrations,
launch-date optimization (universal day + personal day alignment, capped 120-day scan).

These scores are heuristics for reflection and timing preference, never claims
about legal or financial outcomes (spec §24).
